Hi there! I am Valentin, a research engineer in forest economics at [UMR Silva](https://silva.nancy.hub.inrae.fr) ([AgroParisTech](https://www.agroparistech.fr)), and a research associate at the [UMR BETA](https://www.beta-economics.fr), based in [Nancy, France](https://www.google.fr/maps/place/Nancy/@48.6880556,6.1528394,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x4794986e17a692cd:0x4ed671b10d82498d!8m2!3d48.692054!4d6.184417!16zL20vMDExdzRu?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI0MTIwNC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D).

My research lies at the intersection of forest economics, international economics, economic geography, and urban economics. I use applied mathematics and data science—particularly graph theory and machine learning—alongside GIS and large-scale datasets ([FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#home), [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org)) to study the structure and dynamics of the forest-wood sector, with a focus on trade and the forest-based bioeconomy.

In addition to my research, I coordinate the [Forest Information and Resource Strategies](https://www.agroparistech.fr/formations-ingenieur/3a-dominante-forest-information-and-resource-strategies) (FIRS) major (M.Sc.2) at AgroParisTech, where I also teach forest economics, with a focus on international trade in wood products. You can learn more about the FIRS major in [this video](https://emedia.paris-saclay.fr/videos/presentation-firs_4zr04sgx0i/).
